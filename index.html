HOW TO PLAY

Learn it in a second. Feel it for weeks.

1

Tap to switch orbits

Your ball races around a ring. One tap anywhere and it jumps to the other orbit: that’s the whole control.

2

Jump on the beat

Spikes come with the music. Tap in time to slip between them, and catch the sparks beside them to push your combo up to x5.

3

Reach the last beat

Survive to the end of the song to clear the level. Catch every gem with 90% of your jumps on the beat, and the star gives you its crown.

The story

Meet Blip

Blip is the last heartbeat of the Great Star. The Silence froze the sky, so he rolls along the orbits in time with the music, and every song he plays to the end wakes a star.

How his world works

  • Orbits are the grooves of a song: it only plays while someone rolls along them.
  • Spikes are stars frozen by the Silence. Touch one and the music stops, but the beat can always start again.
  • Sparks are living notes, and gems are pieces of songs. Catch them up close and the song shines brighter.
  • Play a star’s song to the very end and it wakes up. Play it perfectly and it gives you a crown.

Characters

Blip

A living beat with the Great Star’s song inside. Curious, brave and a little clumsy: he cries easily, but never gives up.

The Great Star

Blip’s mum. She gave him her last song and now sleeps in the ice far away. Her lullaby is the game’s main theme.

The Moon

The sleepy keeper of the slow song. He hides in his dreams, afraid the music will end if he wakes.

Vega

The neon star of Neon City and the queen of speed. She has never lost a race, and she is Blip’s rival turned friend.

The Silence

Not a monster, just the absence of music: a cold violet mist that freezes stars into spikes.
