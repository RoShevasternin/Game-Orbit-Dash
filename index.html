How to play

How to play

Tap to switch

Your ball runs along a ring. One tap anywhere and it jumps to the other orbit. That is the whole control scheme.

Graze for combo

Pass close to a spike without touching it and your combo climbs. The closer you dare, the richer every gem becomes.

Bank the gems

Gems you pick up become ◆ — they buy skins, permanent upgrades and new levels. Hit a spike and the run ends, so know when to stop.
