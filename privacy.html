Who we are

Orbit Dash is made by Lewydo, a two-person independent game studio from Ukraine (Vlad and Lilya). In this policy “we” means Lewydo, “the app” means the Orbit Dash Android application, and “this site” means https://roshevasternin.github.io/Game-Orbit-Dash/, including the browser version of the game.

Questions, requests, complaints: roshevasternin@gmail.com. We answer in person — there is no support department.

The short version. This website never shows you ads. It counts visits with Google Analytics only if you tap Allow in the small banner — until then, and if you tap Decline, nothing is sent to Google and no cookies are set; the fonts and every other file come from this site itself. The Android app is free and pays for itself with ads, so it does share some data with Google and TikTok; everything it shares is listed below. Your game progress lives on your own device.

This website and the browser version

This site is plain static files on GitHub Pages. We do not run advertising, social widgets or other third-party code here. Fonts, images, sound and the game itself are served from this site, so your browser does not contact anyone else while you read or play — unless you allow Google Analytics (see “Website analytics” below) or open RANKS in the game (see below).

  • No cookies unless you allow analytics. We set none of our own; Google Analytics sets its cookies only after you tap Allow.
  • Blip's cartoon episodes play in YouTube's own player (from youtube-nocookie.com, YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode), and only after you tap ▶ on an episode. Nothing is loaded from YouTube before that tap. Once the player is open, YouTube's privacy policy applies to it; closing the player removes it from the page.
  • Your progress in the browser version (best score, gems, skins, settings) is written to your browser's localStorage on your own device. It never reaches us, and it is separate from the Android app's progress. Clearing your browser data erases it.
  • Your language choice is remembered the same way, in your browser only.
  • RANKS in the browser version (web leaderboards). Nothing is sent until you open RANKS; then the game loads the web boards from our own server (Google Cloud / Firebase, EU region). Only if you tap JOIN RANKS does our server store the nickname you type, your country flag (you can hide it), your orb (ball, trail and colours) and the results of your runs, linked to a random ID kept in your browser. Your nickname, flag, orb and results are shown publicly on the web boards, which are separate from the app's. Nicknames are checked automatically and replaced if they break the rules. To keep the boards fair, joining and sending results use Google reCAPTCHA through Firebase App Check, which may set Google's own cookie (Google's privacy policy); we look up your country from your connection and do not keep your IP address. Profiles not used for 24 months are deleted. To delete yours sooner, use RANKS → ⓘ → DELETE MY RANKS PROFILE, or write to roshevasternin@gmail.com with your nickname.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the files. Like any web host, GitHub processes the technical request data (such as your IP address) needed to deliver a page. See GitHub's privacy statement. We receive no reports or statistics from it.

Website analytics (Google Analytics)

To see how many people play Orbit Dash in the browser and which parts of the game they enjoy, this site can use Google Analytics 4 (run by Google). It is off until you choose: a small banner asks “Can we count visits with Google Analytics?”. Before you answer, and if you tap Decline, Google Analytics is not loaded at all — no request goes to Google and no cookie is set. Only if you tap Allow does your browser load Google's script (googletagmanager.com) and send visit data to google-analytics.com.

  • Where: on this page and in the browser version of the game (/play/). Not in the live game preview that other sites embed, and not on this policy page.
  • What is collected: pages viewed; that a game, a run or a level started; the level's name and whether it was finished; a rough score range at the end of a run (such as “500–999”, never the exact score); opening RANKS and joining it; clicks on the Google Play / App Store buttons. Plus what Google Analytics records with any visit (including its standard “enhanced measurement”, such as how far a page was scrolled and clicks on links to other sites): a random browser ID, the page address, the referring site, the time, and your browser, device type, language and approximate location (country / city), which Google derives from your IP address and does not store. We do not send your name, e-mail, RANKS nickname or anything you type.
  • Cookies: _ga and _ga_KDLF8656QS (a random ID and the current session), first-party on this site's path, kept for up to 13 months. Your answer itself is remembered in your browser's localStorage (lewydo:od-analytics); after 13 months we ask again.
  • No advertising: Google signals and ad personalisation are switched off for this site; the data is used only for statistics.
  • Retention: Google Analytics keeps the event data for 2 months (the setting of our Analytics property); the random browser ID itself can be kept up to 14 months from your last visit and is then deleted.
  • Changing your mind: use Cookie settings at the bottom of the main page, PRIVACY OPTIONS in the game's Settings, or the button below. If you switch from Allow to Decline, the script stops sending and the cookies are deleted.
  • Legal basis: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time. Google's own handling is described in Google's privacy policy and how Google uses data from sites that use its services.

This is the website's own analytics. The apps' analytics are separate and are described in their own section below (one policy, a separate section per platform).

The Android app

The app does not ask you to create an account and does not ask for your name, e-mail, photos, contacts, files or location. Your saved game — scores, gems, skins, upgrades, daily missions, level progress — is stored on your device. We do not have a server and never see it.

Your choices

  • Ads: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android → Settings → Privacy → Ads, and opt out of ad personalisation there. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you can reopen the consent form with Privacy options in the game's Settings and change your answer at any time. The one-time Remove Ads purchase turns off the banner and the ads on opening the game; rewarded ads stay, and you only see them when you choose to.
  • Notifications and vibration: off in the system settings and in the game.
  • Leaderboards: do not sign in to Play Games, and nothing is published.
  • Everything else: uninstalling the app stops all collection. Data already sent to Google or TikTok is handled under their policies, linked above.

Children

Orbit Dash is not directed at children under 13 (or the higher age your country sets). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the app, write to roshevasternin@gmail.com and we will delete what we can and ask our providers to do the same.

How long data is kept

Apart from the web leaderboards described above, we keep no database of our own. Analytics and crash data are kept by Google for the retention period set in our Firebase / Google Analytics project: analytics events (from the app and, if you allowed it, from this website) for 2 months, the random analytics ID up to 14 months after the last activity, crash reports for a shorter time; TikTok keeps attribution data under its own policy. Your game progress stays on your device for as long as the app or the browser data is there.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EEA/UK, or the CCPA/CPRA in California) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. We do not sell or share personal data for cross-context behavioural advertising in the CCPA sense beyond the ad personalisation you can switch off as described above.

To exercise any right, write to roshevasternin@gmail.com. Because we hold no account data for the app, we will usually need you to tell us which device and roughly when you played so we can pass a request on to the provider. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

International transfers and security

Our providers are global companies and may process data outside your country, including outside the EEA, using the transfer safeguards described in their policies (such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses). All traffic from the app and this site uses encrypted connections.
