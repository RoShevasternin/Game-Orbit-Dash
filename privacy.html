Website analytics (Google Analytics)

To see how many people play Orbit Dash in the browser and which parts of the game they enjoy, this site can use Google Analytics 4 (run by Google). It is off until you choose: a small banner asks “Can we count visits with Google Analytics?”. Before you answer, and if you tap Decline, Google Analytics is not loaded at all — no request goes to Google and no cookie is set. Only if you tap Allow does your browser load Google's script (googletagmanager.com) and send visit data to google-analytics.com.

  • Where: on this page and in the browser version of the game (/play/). Not in the live game preview that other sites embed, and not on this policy page.
  • What is collected: pages viewed; that a game, a run or a level started; the level's name and whether it was finished; a rough score range at the end of a run (such as “500–999”, never the exact score); opening RANKS and joining it; clicks on the Google Play / App Store buttons. Plus what Google Analytics records with any visit (including its standard “enhanced measurement”, such as how far a page was scrolled and clicks on links to other sites): a random browser ID, the page address, the referring site, the time, and your browser, device type, language and approximate location (country / city), which Google derives from your IP address and does not store. We do not send your name, e-mail, RANKS nickname or anything you type.
  • Cookies: _ga and _ga_KDLF8656QS (a random ID and the current session), first-party on this site's path, kept for up to 13 months. Your answer itself is remembered in your browser's localStorage (lewydo:od-analytics); after 13 months we ask again.
  • No advertising: Google signals and ad personalisation are switched off for this site; the data is used only for statistics.
  • Retention: Google Analytics keeps the event data for 2 months (the setting of our Analytics property); the random browser ID itself can be kept up to 14 months from your last visit and is then deleted.
  • Changing your mind: use Cookie settings at the bottom of the main page, PRIVACY OPTIONS in the game's Settings, or the button below. If you switch from Allow to Decline, the script stops sending and the cookies are deleted.
  • Legal basis: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time. Google's own handling is described in Google's privacy policy and how Google uses data from sites that use its services.

The Android app

You do not need an account to play, and the app does not ask for your name, e-mail, photos, contacts, files or location. Your saved game — scores, gems, skins, upgrades, daily missions, level progress — is stored on your device; we see none of it unless you join RANKS, which sends your records and your orb from it. The app contacts our own server only for RANKS: it downloads the public boards when you open them and, about once a day in the background, a short summary of them, even if you never open RANKS. The summary it asks for is the world's and that of the country your phone's region and language settings suggest (or the one shown on the JOIN RANKS screen, once you have opened it) — that country is the only thing these downloads say about you, and since 26 October 2026 (18:00 UTC) the server no longer logs them with your IP address; a profile is stored only if you join (see RANKS in the app). If you have joined RANKS, the app also collects from our server the gems your weekly rings have won when you start the game or come back to it after at least 30 seconds away, even if you don't open RANKS; it sends this request only when it knows that gems are waiting for you.

RANKS in the app (Android and iPhone)

RANKS are the game's own leaderboards: weekly and all-time boards for your country and the world. They are optional. You can look at the boards without joining — the game downloads them as public files that carry nothing about you beyond which board you ask for. Even if you never open RANKS, the app downloads a short public summary of the world's boards and of the country your phone's region and language settings suggest (or the one shown on the JOIN RANKS screen, once you have opened it), in the background and about once a day, so that it can invite you to RANKS after GAME OVER. Apart from that suggested country, these downloads carry nothing about you, and since 26 October 2026 (18:00 UTC) the server no longer logs them with your IP address (see How long RANKS data is kept). No profile is stored until you tap JOIN RANKS.

What our server stores when you join:

  • Your nickname. Either your Google Play Games / Game Center player name after an automatic filter (it removes emoji, invisible characters, contact details and similar, and shortens long names), or a name generated from our own word list. You cannot type a free-form name. If Game Center marks your account as belonging to a minor, you always get a generated name.
  • Your country. The game suggests your country from your internet connection (and your phone's region and language settings). When you join or change your country, the game also sends your phone's regions and the game's language so that the server can check the choice; they are not stored, but your profile notes where the country came from (your connection, your phone or the language). Our server looks the country up in a database it keeps itself (IP to Country Lite by DB-IP, CC BY 4.0) and keeps only the two-letter country code in your profile — never your IP address. Since 26 October 2026 (18:00 UTC) the server's standard request logs (Google Cloud), which used to record the IP address of each request, are no longer stored; entries from before then are deleted after 30 days, by 26 November 2026 (see How long RANKS data is kept). You can change your country once every 30 days, or hide your flag at any time (you then appear only on the world boards, with a globe instead of a flag).
  • Your results and your orb: your scores and totals on the boards (BEST, GEMS, SPARKS, CRASHES — this week and all time), the identifiers and results of your latest runs (up to 20, for up to 24 hours, to check them and to avoid counting a run twice; a run sent before it had ended is kept for each installation until that installation sends again), your orb (ball, trail, orbit shape and anthem), the rings and HONORS you win, and the reports you send or receive. When you join, the game also sends the records already in your saved game (best score, total sparks and number of runs), so that your ALL TIME boards start from them. A run held for a fair-play check (for example, an unusually high score) stays in your profile with its score, gems, sparks and duration until it is confirmed or rejected, for up to 8 days, and the automatic moderation records keep what was checked (see How long RANKS data is kept). To spot suspicious records, your profile also keeps a summary of your endless runs: how many, their average and spread, and your top 3 scores.
  • Gems from rings. A ring you win on a weekly board can come with gems for the game. Until the game collects them, the server keeps the gems that are waiting for each week (the latest 8 such weeks). The game collects them when you open RANKS, when it shows your weekly results, and when you start the game or come back to it after at least 30 seconds away — even if you don't open RANKS, but only when it knows that gems are waiting. Each such request carries your RANKS sign-in, the week and a random one-time code made on your device. For each collected week the server keeps the week, the gems and that code (the latest 16 weeks), and it counts these requests for the current day (at most 20 a day).
  • Identifiers. To confirm it is really you, the game uses your Google Play Games sign-in on Android or Game Center on iPhone. We store only a one-way code derived from that account (a keyed hash) — never its ID, photo or friends. Without Play Games or Game Center, the game creates a random key on your device, and we store a one-way code of that key instead. We also store a random public player ID (used in the boards' data instead of any account ID) and random IDs of up to five installations of the game that use your profile.
  • App integrity. Before it talks to the RANKS server, the game proves it is the genuine app on a real device: Google Play Integrity on Android, Apple App Attest (or DeviceCheck) on iPhone, both through Firebase App Check. The resulting short-lived token goes to Google and to our server and is not stored in our database. Deleting your profile never depends on this check (see Deleting your RANKS profile).

Who sees it: your nickname, country flag (unless hidden), orb, results, rings and HONORS are shown to all players on the boards and on your player card. Your account codes, installation IDs and reports are never shown.

Why: what our server stores is used only to run RANKS and keep them fair. Legal bases: performance of the feature you asked for by joining (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)) and our legitimate interest in preventing cheating and in keeping the server secure and working, which is also the basis for the server's logs (Art. 6(1)(f)). Your RANKS profile is not used for ads, and your RANKS nickname, country and identifiers, and the players you report, are never sent to analytics. The app's own analytics is separate: on Android, Firebase Analytics receives the game's gameplay events — including the score, gems and sparks of every run, whether or not you join RANKS — and counts how RANKS is used, linked to random IDs of your installation rather than to your RANKS profile (see The Android app).

Where: on our RANKS server, which runs on Google Cloud / Firebase (Firestore and Cloud Functions in the europe-west1 region, Belgium); the public board files are delivered through Firebase Hosting's content delivery network. Google processes this data for us under its data processing terms.

Fair play and moderation. Any player can report a nickname or an unfair score (tap a player → REPORT); the reported player is hidden on the reporter's device at once. Reports from established players are counted automatically: after enough independent reports, an offensive nickname is replaced with a generated name for everyone, and a suspicious score is hidden from the boards until it no longer looks suspicious. Accounts caught cheating can be blocked.

Deleting your RANKS profile

You can delete your RANKS profile, and the data linked to it, at any time.

  • In the game: open RANKS, tap ⓘ (how RANKS work) and, at the bottom, DELETE MY RANKS PROFILE. You need an internet connection, but not the app-integrity check: deleting works even if your device fails it. The game only has to show that the profile is yours — with your Google Play Games or Game Center sign-in or, if you joined without them, with the random key the game keeps on your device. If you have reinstalled the game or cleared its data and you joined with Google Play Games or Game Center, sign in with the same account, open RANKS and tap JOIN RANKS first: the game finds your existing profile instead of creating a new one (this works without the integrity check too), and DELETE MY RANKS PROFILE appears under ⓘ. The game deletes only the profile of the sign-in you use now: if you have signed out of Google Play Games or Game Center or switched to another account, it says so instead of deleting (and, if that sign-in has a profile of its own, shows it). To delete the other profile, sign back in with the account you joined with and delete it there. If you joined without them, the random key is gone after a reinstall; write to us instead (below). The browser version has its own RANKS profile; delete it the same way in the browser where you joined.
  • Without the game (for example, if it is no longer installed): write to roshevasternin@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my RANKS profile”, your nickname, your country flag (or that it was hidden) and whether you played on Android, iPhone or in the browser. We delete the profile by hand and reply when it is done.

What happens then:

  • At once: your profile is marked as deleted on our server and can no longer be used.
  • Within about 15 minutes: your nickname, flag, orb and results disappear from all boards.
  • Within a day: your place in weekly final results is left empty; cached copies of the public board files and of your HONORS on the content delivery network expire. Other players' devices may still show copies they downloaded earlier: the boards until they are refreshed, and a weekly final result for up to a week.
  • Within 2 days: the profile record itself is erased from our database — an automatic clean-up removes it a day after you delete it, and the clean-up itself can take up to another day.
  • Kept longer: automatic moderation records that mention the profile expire after 90 days; server request logs from before 26 October 2026 (18:00 UTC) expire after 30 days, by 26 November 2026; if the profile was blocked for cheating, the one-way account code is kept to stop the same account from coming back.
  • Not affected: your game progress on your device, and your Google Play Games or Game Center account, which Google and Apple manage.

Your choices

  • Ads: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android → Settings → Privacy → Ads, and opt out of ad personalisation there. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you can reopen the consent form with Privacy options in the game's Settings and change your answer at any time. The one-time Remove Ads purchase turns off the banner and the ads on opening the game; rewarded ads stay, and you only see them when you choose to.
  • Notifications and vibration: off in the system settings and in the game.
  • Leaderboards: do not sign in to Play Games (Android) or Game Center (iPhone: Settings → Game Center) and do not join RANKS, and nothing is published. In RANKS you can hide your flag, pick a generated name, or delete your profile.
  • Everything else: uninstalling the app stops all collection (a RANKS profile stays until you delete it or 24 months pass). Data already sent to Google or TikTok is handled under their policies, linked above.

Children

Orbit Dash is not directed at children under 13 (or the higher age your country sets). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the app, write to roshevasternin@gmail.com and we will delete what we can and ask our providers to do the same.

How long data is kept

The only database of our own is the RANKS server; how long it keeps data is described in How long RANKS data is kept (the browser version's RANKS work the same way). Analytics and crash data are kept by Google for the retention period set in our Firebase / Google Analytics project: analytics events (from the app and, if you allowed it, from this website) for 2 months, the random analytics ID up to 14 months after the last activity, crash reports for a shorter time; TikTok keeps attribution data under its own policy. Your game progress stays on your device for as long as the app or the browser data is there.
