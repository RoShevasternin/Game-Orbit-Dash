About the game

Two orbits. One tap. Every jump on the beat.

Orbit Dash is a neon rhythm arcade in deep space. Tap to switch between two orbits and slip past the spikes in time with the music.

Every level is built on its own original track, from a calm first light to a pipe-organ DEMON finale. The beat tells you when to jump: miss it and the spikes catch you, ride it and the whole galaxy lights up. Practise from checkpoints, then play it for real from the very start and go for the crown: every gem, every jump on the beat.

Between songs there is the Endless mode, where the rings keep speeding up, daily missions with a seven-day streak, and weekly leaderboards for your country and the world. In VERSUS you race friends or players from around the world live, on the same level at the same time.

  • All music is original. Every level and menu theme is written for the game.
  • One-tap controls. Easy to learn, hard to master, made for phones.
  • VERSUS live races. Play the same level against friends or the world.
  • Leaderboards and weekly tournaments for your country and the world.
  • Endless mode, daily missions, trails and orbit styles to collect.
  • 82 languages The game speaks the player’s language.
